How to play

  1. Tickets drop on the rail. Each one is a recipe and a clock.
  2. Tap ingredients in order onto the plate. Undo or dump if you miss.
  3. Hit Fire when the plate matches any open ticket.
  4. Faster tickets and combos beat Line B. Service lasts 90 seconds.

Keyboard: 1–8 add ingredients, Enter fires, Backspace undoes, Esc dumps.
