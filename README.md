# Competitive Kitchen

A phone meal planner in the spirit of apps like mise: pick a store and a weekly budget, say what you already have, get a photo meal plan and a checkable grocery list.

Live: [competitive-kitchen.vercel.app](https://competitive-kitchen.vercel.app)

## Tabs

- **Plan** — estimated cost vs budget, one meal card per cooking day, leftover lunch noted
- **Meals** — browse by cuisine, open a recipe for macros and ingredients
- **List** — grocery list sorted by category, best typical price at your store, maps link
- **Prefs** — supermarket, budget, household size, cooking days, cravings, pantry

Prices are typical US estimates for Aldi / Walmart / Kroger / Whole Foods, not a live local ad feed.
